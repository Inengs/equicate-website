12-Week Intensive Curriculum

Comprehensive, exam-focused preparation for Mathematics and Physics designed to maximize WASSCE and JAMB UTME scores.

Week 1 • Free Month

Foundational Algebra

Number bases, modular arithmetic, indices & laws of indices, introduction to logarithms.

Week 2 • Free Month

Surds & Sets

Surds simplification, standard forms, approximations, Venn diagrams, set operations.

Week 3 • Free Month

Equations & Expressions

Algebraic simplification, simple linear equations, simultaneous linear equations.

Week 4 • Diagnostic

Quadratics & Assessment

Quadratic equations, word problems, plus full Diagnostic Test & Mock Quiz.

Week 5 • Paid Phase

Sequences & Series

Arithmetic Progressions (A.P.) and Geometric Progressions (G.P.) applications.

Week 6 • Paid Phase

Variations & Matrices

Direct, inverse, joint, and partial variation; introduction to matrices and determinants.

Week 7 • Paid Phase

Plane Geometry

Angles, polygon properties, circle theorems, and geometric constructions.

Week 8 • Assessment

Coordinate Geometry & Mensuration

Perimeter, area, volume calculations, plus Month 2 Benchmark Evaluation.

Week 9 • Paid Phase

Trigonometry

Ratios, sine & cosine rules, angles of elevation/depression, bearings.

Week 10 • Paid Phase

Statistics & Probability

Mean, median, mode, standard deviation, and probability calculations.

Week 11 • Paid Phase

Calculus & Vectors

Introductory differentiation, integration, vectors, and simple transformations.

Week 12 • Final Exam

Full WASSCE / JAMB Mock

Timed past-question exam practice, weak-topic clinic, and exam readiness review.

Week 1 • Free Month

Mechanics & Motion

Measurements, units, scalars & vectors, motion in a straight line, equations of motion.

Week 2 • Free Month

Forces & Newton's Laws

Newton's laws of motion, friction, and introductory diagnostic testing.

Week 3 • Free Month

Work, Energy & Power

Simple machines, mechanical advantage, efficiency, and energy conservation.

Week 4 • Diagnostic

Equilibrium & Projectiles

Moments, centre of gravity, projectile motion, and 1st Month Mock Assessment.

Week 5 • Paid Phase

Thermal Physics

Heat energy, expansion, gas laws (Boyle's/Charles's), and thermometry.

Week 6 • Paid Phase

Waves & Sound

Properties of wave motion, sound propagation, echoes, and resonance.

Week 7 • Paid Phase

Optics & Light

Reflection, refraction, lenses, optical instruments, and dispersion.

Week 8 • Assessment

Electricity & Electrostatics

Coulomb's law, Ohm's law, electrical circuits, plus Month 2 Evaluation.

Week 9 • Paid Phase

Magnetism

Magnetic fields, electromagnetic induction, generators, and transformers.

Week 10 • Paid Phase

Basic Electronics

Semiconductors, p-n junction diodes, transistors, and application circuits.

Week 11 • Paid Phase

Atomic & Nuclear Physics

Atomic structure, radioactivity, half-life, and nuclear reactions.

Week 12 • Final Exam

Comprehensive Mock Exam

Full WASSCE/JAMB physics mock simulation, past-question clinics, and feedback.
